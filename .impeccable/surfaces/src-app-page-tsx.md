# SwachhGrid surfaces

Scope: landing `/` (Persuade), resident app `/report`, crew app `/worker`, command centre `/command` (Operate). One visual world across all four.
Audience first: hackathon judges on laptop and phone; flows stay fully usable for residents, crews, officers.
Memorable moment: the painted CLEARED / साफ stamp landing on a job when the crew proves the clean.
Unresolved: shared database; real zone boundaries.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every report is a painted municipal notice, and the city answers in the same paint. Refuses the dark-glass civic dashboard with one neon accent and the green-saffron government portal.

OWN-WORLD: Flat enamel sign paint at full strength: chrome yellow #F2B705, vermilion #D7372B, municipal board green #14513A, night board indigo #1C2340, paint black #16130F, letter white #F4F1E8. Painted pinstripe frames on boards, Yatra One painted Devanagari/Latin display, Teko condensed for numerals and sign labels, Mukta for body. Rubber-stamp vermilion ink for proof. No glass, no gradients, no tiles of icon cards.

STORY: The judge sees real Nagpur roofs and live open reports at once, understands that each report gets an owner, a deadline and photo proof, and opens the resident, crew or command view to try it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing splits the screen: a chrome-yellow signboard (left, ~46%) with the painted Marathi line "येथे कचरा टाकू नये." at display scale, an English answer line, and two painted actions (Report a problem; Open the command centre); a hard-cropped duotone (ink + yellow) satellite of Mominpura (right) with live open reports as vermilion paint dots and a hanging painted tally of open / past-deadline counts.

FORM: Painted Notice, position 5 on the ordered list, seed key 3a0b3ffb. Raises: provenance custody line on every report; zones as a row of identical meters; flat colour at page scale; one snapping stamp moment. Signature interaction: the stamp. Motion grammar: still paint; only the stamp and live arrivals move, exponential ease-out, reduced motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
